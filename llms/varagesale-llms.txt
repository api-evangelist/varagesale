# VarageSale

> VarageSale is a community-based online marketplace for buying and selling used items locally. Founded in 2012 by Tami Zuckerman and Carl Mercier and headquartered in Toronto, it connects verified neighbors in local communities through iOS/Android apps and the web. Acquired by VerticalScope in 2017; MaxSold assumed operational control in early 2026. VarageSale is a direct-to-consumer marketplace and does not publish a public developer API.
